## Top stories

### Democrats win the House and Senate
Networks project Democrats won both chambers. They hold about 225 House seats (218 needed) and flipped Senate seats in Texas, North Carolina and South Carolina. James Talarico is the first Texas Democrat to win a Senate seat since 1988. Counts are provisional.

### Talk of replacing Chief of Staff Susie Wiles
Reports say Trump asked allies about replacing Wiles, naming Stephen Miller, Bill Pulte and Lee Zeldin as options. The White House denies it.
<br>[Voz](https://voz.us/en/politics/261017/40044/)

### Bank of England sees small AI gains
It estimates AI will lift UK productivity about 0.5% over three years. Firms are cutting hiring, not staff.
