Democrats take Congress; AI stocks take the hit

The midterm results set the week. Markets had their worst day in months on Thursday, then partly recovered Friday.

Top stories

Democrats win control of the House and Senate

Networks projected Democrats won both chambers in Tuesday's midterms. Reported House totals are around 225 seats, with 218 needed. Senate pickups include Texas, where James Talarico became the first Democrat to win a Senate seat there since 1988, plus North Carolina and South Carolina. Final seat counts differ between outlets while counting continues. Democrats won't have veto-proof majorities.

ABC News · Outlook

Pressure builds for a White House shake-up

After the losses, reports say President Trump has asked allies about replacing Chief of Staff Susie Wiles. Stephen Miller, Bill Pulte and Lee Zeldin have been named as possible successors. The White House calls the reports false. No change has been confirmed.

Voz, citing Politico and The Washington Post

UK central bank sees small AI productivity gains

The Bank of England estimates AI will lift UK productivity by about 0.5% over three years. It says firms are mostly cutting hiring rather than laying people off.
