Trump names Stephen Miller White House chief of staff

President Trump said Miller, now deputy chief of staff, takes over from Susie Wiles starting Monday, Nov. 9. Wiles moves to Trump's political operation. Al Jazeera tied the change to Republicans' heavy midterm losses. Miller has been a longtime aide and a central figure in immigration policy.

Herzog gives Eisenkot the first chance to form Israel's government

On Sunday, Israeli President Isaac Herzog formally tasked Gadi Eisenkot. Parties with 67 Knesset seats recommended Eisenkot, and parties with 46 backed Netanyahu. Eisenkot has 28 days to build a coalition, with a possible 14-day extension. Herzog cited mutual vetoes between and within the blocs, so a coalition is not assured.

COP31 climate talks open in Antalya, Turkey

The UN climate conference runs Nov. 9–20. Turkey hosts, and Australia leads the negotiations, a split arrangement for the first time. Agenda items include adaptation goals, national climate pledges and climate finance. These sources are previews, not reports from the opening session.
